# SOP-024: Ambient Condition Check

**Applies when:** temperature rose on several machines together, moving in the same direction on every machine, with a mild defect rise across machines, or operators mention ambient heat, an AC unit trip, HVAC, open bay doors, room temperature or humidity. Environment hypothesis.

## Verification steps
1. Record ambient temperature and humidity near the affected machines.
2. Check HVAC and AC unit status and its log for the incident period.
3. Confirm bay door and ventilation positions during the incident.
4. Compare the machine temperature rise with ambient readings over the same period.

## Expected observations
An environmental issue usually shows a similar temperature rise on all machines at the same time, tracking ambient conditions. A rise on one machine only points to that machine instead.

## Escalation
Escalate to facilities and maintenance engineering before any corrective action.
