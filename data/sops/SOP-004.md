# SOP-004: Abnormal Vibration Investigation

**Applies when:** vibration rose on a machine compared with its baseline, motor current rose, speed became unstable (speed jitter), high-vibration alarms (ALM_VIB_HI) occur, or operators report grinding noise, rattle or a rough bearing. Mechanical machine hypothesis on the drive, gearbox or bearings.

## Verification steps
1. Take a handheld vibration reading at the drive end and compare it with the baseline vibration level.
2. Inspect bearings and gearbox for noise, play or heat, and record the findings.
3. Compare motor current with its baseline for the same period.
4. Confirm whether speed instability coincides with the vibration rise on the same machine.

## Expected observations
A mechanical issue usually shows vibration and motor current rising together on one machine, often with unstable speed and a noise report. Vibration that stays at baseline does not support a mechanical hypothesis.

## Escalation
Escalate to maintenance engineering before any corrective action. Do not run the machine at higher load while verification is pending.
