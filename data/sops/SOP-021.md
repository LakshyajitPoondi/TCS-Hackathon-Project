# SOP-021: Shift Handover and Calibration Check

**Applies when:** a shift change (SHIFT_CHG) occurs shortly before the defect rise, sensors stay normal, or operator notes mention a handover, an incomplete checklist, a new operator or a skipped calibration check. People (handover or procedure) hypothesis.

## Verification steps
1. Review the shift handover checklist for incomplete items.
2. Verify that the required calibration checks were completed at shift start and recorded.
3. Confirm the incoming operator's training record for the affected machine.
4. Compare defect counts in the hour before and after the handover.

## Expected observations
A people or procedure issue usually shows defects rising soon after the shift change while machine sensors stay at baseline, often with a note about the handover or calibration.

## Escalation
Escalate to the shift supervisor and quality engineering before any corrective action.
