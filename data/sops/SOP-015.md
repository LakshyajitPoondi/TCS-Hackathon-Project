# SOP-015: Sensor Verification

**Applies when:** a temperature reading flat-lined (identical readings) or shows isolated spikes or non-physical jumps, while vibration, motor current and speed stay within baseline and defects stay near baseline. Operators may report a stuck panel display or a panel reading that differs from a handheld probe. Measurement (sensor) hypothesis.

## Verification steps
1. Compare the panel reading with a calibrated handheld instrument or housing probe at the same point.
2. Inspect the sensor wiring and connector for damage or looseness, and record the findings.
3. Check the sensor's calibration record and calibration date.
4. Confirm whether the flat-line or spike pattern repeats during the comparison.

## Expected observations
A measurement issue shows an unusual reading on one sensor that no other signal corroborates: other sensors and defect counts stay at baseline. If independent signals also changed, the reading is more likely real.

## Escalation
Escalate to instrumentation or maintenance engineering before any corrective action based on the suspect reading.
