# SOP-007: Cooling System Troubleshooting

**Applies when:** temperature rose on one machine compared with its baseline (elevated temperature), speed was reduced, high-temperature alarms (ALM_TEMP_HI) occur, or operators report a hot housing, weak cooling fan, low coolant flow or poor airflow. Cooling-related machine hypothesis on a single machine.

## Verification steps
1. Check coolant level and chiller flow on the affected machine and record the values.
2. Inspect the cooling fan and heat-exchanger fins for blockage or reduced airflow.
3. Record barrel and housing temperature over ten minutes and compare it with the machine's normal running temperature.
4. Confirm whether the speed reduction coincides with the temperature rise on the same machine.

## Expected observations
A cooling issue usually shows a gradual temperature rise on one machine only, with reduced speed and high-temperature alarms. A rise on all machines together points to ambient conditions instead.

## Escalation
Escalate to maintenance engineering before any corrective action on the cooling circuit.
