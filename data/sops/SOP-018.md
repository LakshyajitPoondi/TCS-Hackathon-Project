# SOP-018: Changeover Verification

**Applies when:** a changeover (CHG) event occurs shortly before the defect rise, a new part or recipe was loaded, setpoints changed, speed shifted on the changed machine, or the defect rise is confined to one machine. Method (changeover or setup) hypothesis.

## Verification steps
1. Compare the loaded setpoints against the approved setup sheet for the new part.
2. Confirm that the first-off part inspection after the changeover was completed and recorded.
3. Review the changeover checklist for skipped or incomplete items.
4. Record the speed setpoint before and after the changeover on the affected machine.

## Expected observations
A method issue usually shows a defect rise on the changed machine soon after the changeover, sometimes with a speed setpoint shift, while other machines stay normal.

## Escalation
Escalate to process engineering before any corrective action or setpoint change.
