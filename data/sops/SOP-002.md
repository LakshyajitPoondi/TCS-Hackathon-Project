# SOP-002: Downtime and Alarm Triage

**Applies when:** alarms are raised during the incident window (ALM_TEMP_HI high-temperature alarm, ALM_VIB_HI high-vibration alarm), downtime or STOP events increase, a QC_HOLD is recorded, or the event log shows repeated events on a machine. General triage for any incident.

## Verification steps
1. Review the alarm and downtime log for the incident window and record each alarm code, machine and time.
2. Confirm each alarm was acknowledged and that its trigger condition was recorded by the operator.
3. Compare downtime and STOP entries against the production log to check the recorded stop reasons.
4. Record whether alarms repeat on the same machine or spread across several machines.

## Expected observations
Alarm codes, times and machines line up with the sensor deviations in the window. Each downtime entry has a recorded reason. Alarms that repeat on one machine point the investigation to that machine; alarms on several machines point to a shared condition.

## Escalation
Escalate to the shift supervisor if alarms were not acknowledged, and to maintenance engineering before any corrective action.
