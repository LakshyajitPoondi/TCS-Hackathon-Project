# SOP-012: Defect Analysis and Batch Check

**Applies when:** defects are concentrated in one batch or lot, the same batch ran on several machines, sensors stayed normal while defects rose, or operators mention a new supplier, resin, lot or off-spec material. Material hypothesis.

## Verification steps
1. Compare defect counts by batch and lot across machines for the incident period.
2. Pull retained samples from the suspect lot and inspect them against the material specification.
3. Check the lot's certificate of analysis and incoming-inspection record against spec.
4. Confirm whether other lines or machines received the same lot and record their defect counts.

## Expected observations
A material issue usually shows defects concentrated in one batch across several machines, while temperature, vibration, current and speed stay at baseline. Defects spread evenly across batches do not support a material hypothesis.

## Escalation
Escalate to quality engineering and the supplier quality contact before any corrective action or lot disposition.
